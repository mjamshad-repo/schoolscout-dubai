About & sources

SchoolScout is a retrieval-augmented assistant for parents shortlisting private schools in Dubai. Dubai has over 220 private schools across 17 curricula; fees, KHDA ratings and locations are spread across inspection reports and news lists. SchoolScout puts them in one place and answers questions with citations.

Data in this pilot

  • 22 Outstanding schools: rating and 2026-27 fees from Khaleej Times, 15 July 2026. Fees are frozen for 2026-27.
  • 11 Very Good schools: rating and 2026-27 fees from Gulf News, 6 August 2026.
  • 3 schools use Gulf News, Sept 2022 figures and are marked Fee data from 2022.
  • Areas and ratings for the Outstanding schools are checked against the KHDA Education Directory (28 October 2026).

KHDA ratings

KHDA rates Dubai private schools on a six-point scale: Outstanding, Very Good, Good, Acceptable, Weak, Very Weak. For 2025-26 KHDA paused standard full inspections for most schools and kept existing ratings.

Limits

This is information, not admission advice. Always confirm fees and ratings on khda.gov.ae and with the school before applying.
