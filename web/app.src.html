About & sources

SchoolScout is a retrieval-augmented assistant for parents choosing a private school in Dubai. It puts KHDA's official school register, published fees and distance search in one place, and answers questions with citations.

Data

  • All 236 schools: name, curriculum, overall KHDA rating with inspection year, area, map location, phone, website and inspection report link, from KHDA's private schools register (exported 25 Oct 2026).
  • Fees for 35 schools: 2026-27 fees for the 22 Outstanding schools (Khaleej Times, 15 July 2026) and 11 Very Good schools (Gulf News, 6 August 2026); 2 schools use 2022 figures and are marked Fee data from 2022.
  • Other schools show "Fee not on file". KHDA publishes each school's fees on its Education Directory; check there or with the school.
  • Distances are straight lines between KHDA map points, rounded to about a kilometre. Driving distance is longer.

KHDA ratings

KHDA rates Dubai private schools on a six-point scale: Outstanding, Very Good, Good, Acceptable, Weak, Very Weak. "Not yet inspected" usually means a school is new. Ratings show the year of the inspection they come from.

Limits

Information only, not admission advice. Always confirm fees, seats and ratings with the school and on khda.gov.ae before applying.
